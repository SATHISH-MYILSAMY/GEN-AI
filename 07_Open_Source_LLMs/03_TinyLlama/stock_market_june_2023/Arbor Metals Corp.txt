location in the James Bay region of Quebec.

The company’s stock has had a historical trading volume increase over the last two months as expectations of its exploration program continue to be positive.

Ambitious projects like Volkswagen’s 20 billion lithium-ion battery factory in Ontario signal massive growth for all local lithium mining stocks as the car makers actively seek lithium suppliers. With a promising exploration program for 2023 underway, there are high hopes to unlock the new lithium resources, sharing this lithium growth trend with Patriot and other neighbouring sites.
